## 2. RECENT METHOD DEVELOPMENTS (2024–2026)

**(a) Spatial scheduling citing He/Hong/Kim (J.Sched 2024):** citation trail is thin (3 papers). (i) **Wang & Yin, Frontiers Mar. Sci. 2026 (ICBLSP)** — already in our map; it's the *only* substantive descendant, and it centers removal-obstruction minimization — reinforcing that the crane-lift/LIFO mechanic is the "novel" axis the organizers built on. (ii) Wang et al., "Multi-objective PSO for ship block scheduling", AIITA 2026 — makespan/tardy-count/tardiness/balance objectives (note: near-identical objective vector to OGC's Z1/Z2), low methodological value. (iii) **Integrated dynamic scheduling of shipyard blocks w/ adaptive stockyard layout, Engineering Optimization (Mar 2025)** — https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/0305215X.2025.2466829 — binary-search-tree dynamic layout (guillotine-style free-rectangle tracking) + DRL for integrated schedule+layout; the BST free-space bookkeeping is a cheap alternative to our bit-grid for the rectangle fast path.

**(b) Modern nesting engines — verified, IDs were swapped in our notes:**
- **jagua-rs = arXiv 2508.08341, "Decoupling Geometry from Optimization in 2D Irregular C&P"** (Gardeyn, Vanden Berghe, Wauters; KU Leuven). Open-source Rust CDE, MPL-2.0: https://github.com/JeroenGar/jagua-rs. Techniques worth stealing (reimplement, cite): quadtree hazard indexing, **hazard proximity grid**, **fail-fast surrogates (poles & piers)** — cheap inner-circle/pier proxies that reject most placements before exact polygon tests; feasibility-preserving polygon simplification. Millions of collision queries/sec.
- **sparrow = arXiv 2509.13329, "An open-source heuristic to reboot 2D nesting research"** (same group): https://github.com/JeroenGar/sparrow. Core idea: strip packing as a **sequence of feasibility problems with quantified (weighted) overlap gradually driven to zero** — items may overlap during search; per-pair overlap weights adapt (guided local search flavor); ~2.4M placement samples/sec; beats SOTA "by an unexpectedly wide margin", + 10 new industrial benchmark instances. A 3D fork exists (JonasTollenaere/sparrow-3d). **This is the single most important methodological update for us**: kestrel's packing is construct-only/feasible-only; a sparrow-style "allow overlap, penalize, separate" inner loop on our int64 raster (overlap = popcount of AND of bit-rows — nearly free) is a direct, high-leverage upgrade for congested instances.

**(c) ML-guided packing/scheduling on CPU budgets:** Nothing changes our no-training stance. Hybrid vision-DRL rotational nesting (J. Intell. Manuf. 2025, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10845-025-02620-6) and dense-reward DRL packing (IJSC 2024) beat *open-source nesting software*, not sparrow-class heuristics, and need GPUs/training. GOPT (transformer 3D BPP, arXiv 2409.05344) is online-setting. On a 4-core/16GB few-minute budget with hidden distribution shift, tuned metaheuristics remain the winning play — consistent with both prior OGC winners being ALNS-family, not learned.

**(d) ALNS/matheuristic methodology 2024–2026:**
- **Balans (arXiv 2412.14382)** — multi-armed-bandit ALNS meta-solver over MIP neighborhoods (crossover/mutation/local-branching/RINS/RENS as arms). Directly applicable: run bandit selection over kestrel's destroy operators instead of classic ALNS roulette; Thompson sampling reported best in the related BALANCE study (bandit-based LNS for MAPF, AAAI 2024, anytime setting like ours).
- **DR-ALNS (ICAPS 2024, github.com/RobbertReijnen/DR-ALNS)** — DRL-configured ALNS (operator choice + acceptance temperature); evidence that acceptance-criterion adaptation matters as much as operator choice.
- **Graph-RL neural ALNS framework, COR 2024** (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0305054824002636) and the **VRP-ALNS operator review/ranking (2024, ResearchGate 380857051)** — the review's takeaway: a small set of well-implemented operators + segment-based adaptive weights ≈ the learned variants; operator *diversity* beats operator *count*.
- Q-learning ALNS for docks assignment+scheduling (arXiv 2412.09090) — assignment+schedule coupling like ours; Q-learned operator selection on CPU.

## WHAT CHANGED SINCE OUR STRATEGY DOC

1. **Sparrow's overlap-relaxation paradigm is new SOTA for irregular packing** and maps almost perfectly onto our bit-grid engine (AND+popcount = quantified overlap). Add an "overlap-tolerant separation" mode to the KEEL decoder for congested bays instead of feasible-only construction. This also directly attacks kestrel's known over-constraint: per-layer overlap maps let us drop the union-footprint restriction layer by layer.
2. **Fail-fast surrogates (poles/piers) + hazard proximity grids** (jagua-rs) — cheap pre-checks before exact raster tests; worth adding to the placement sampler if profiling shows collision checks dominate.
3. **Bandit operator selection (Thompson sampling) has overtaken classic ALNS roulette** in 2024-2026 literature (Balans/BALANCE/DR-ALNS); trivial to swap into the unified ALNS, and acceptance-temperature adaptation deserves equal attention.
4. **DMS repo confirms the winning meta-pattern** for this competition series: compiled inner loop + ALNS + exact polish on the assignment-like subproblem. Our KEEL plan already matches; the confirmation raises confidence, and specifically endorses the bounded-Gurobi assignment component (Z2/Z3 are assignment-only — a set-partition/assignment polish over elite solutions is cheap and safe).
5. **CP-SAT is inside the organizers' own toolbox** (Woo lab's 2025 CP papers) — a CP-SAT feasibility/scheduling probe for small congested bays is aligned with what they expect top teams to do, and ortools is whitelisted.
6. ML/RL packing still not competitive under our compute contract — no change to the no-training decision.

## ORGANIZER FINGERPRINT: what they expect / how instances were likely generated

- **Problem DNA = Woo lab**: quay-wall allocation (interval co-presence + position), steel-stockyard rearrangement (removal obstruction ⇒ the crane-lift LIFO rule), plate nesting (polygon layers), block logistics (bay preference/workload). Instances were almost certainly generated from a **DES-style synthetic yard flow**: blocks arrive in a stream (release times from a process), due = release + processing + slack drawn from a distribution, polygons derived from simplified real block outlines (few vertices, mostly convex-ish with notches), bay preferences from a "home shop" assignment. Expect *temporal congestion waves*, not uniformly random geometry.
- **Evaluation DNA = K. Lee / C. Lee / Joung**: IP-literate committee that knows exactly where full spatial MIPs die; ranked scoring with infeasible = -1 plus huge w1 says they expect many teams to fail feasibility on congested hidden instances — **never-infeasible anytime shell is worth more rank than packing brilliance**. Their 2D-knapsack LP-bound work suggests they sized instances so the assignment relaxation (Z2/Z3 + relaxed capacity) is informative — our bounded Gurobi seed targets precisely that structure.
- **Expected solution ladder** (from RUBI + their baselines): (1) urgency-dispatch + bottom-left rectangles, (2) + polygon nesting, (3) + LIFO-aware placement (roofing/overhang exploitation), (4) ALNS over assignment+timing with exact polish. Tier 3 is where the crane-lift rule creates the differentiation the organizers designed for — kestrel's union-footprint over-constraint forfeits exactly that tier; fixing it is the highest-rank-leverage change available.
