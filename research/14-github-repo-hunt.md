- v1 congestion-first construction + ALAP (exit at due date) + burial rule: 1.067B total @120s. ALAP anchoring piles blocks at due dates → overflow → serialized tardiness.
- v2 EDD + dense packing + naive LNS: REGRESSED to 1.658B (dense early packing starves the congested tail; LNS non-monotone).
- v3 KEY INSIGHT: EDD-earliest (earliest-due-first, place at EARLIEST feasible slot >= release, not ALAP) reproduces a relaxed list-schedule's tardiness under real constraints → 442M. Plus multi-start best-of {EDD-earliest, congestion-ALAP, seeded perturbations} for structural no-regression.
- v4/v5: basin-hopping destroy-repair improver (small destroys k<=6, capped repair search, kick after 6 stagnant rounds, diversified repair orderings: EDD/EDD+jitter/due-then-proc/release-then-due; window-destroy mode that evicts ALL blocks in a peak-congestion time window of one bay) → 318M @1worker/150s.
- v6 FAILED: naive candidate-position enrichment (left/bottom contacts) made placements 3x slower and displaced the useful positions past the search cap → 2.4x worse. Lesson: candidate-set tuning interacts with caps.
- v7: exact pairwise geometry memoization — collision, crane-entry, crane-exit cached per (block_id,orient,x,y) pair; exact because utils' check_entry/check_exit are separable per present block and pairwise; verified 24,000-case equivalence → ~1.5x effective rounds.
- v8: widened repair caps (slot_time_cap 18→40, pos_cap 14→30) enabled by caching → 311.2M @1w/100s.
Their analytic tooling (ideas worth replicating independently): (a) "joint floor" = assignment-only relaxation, obj1=0 assumed, multi-start SA on w2·Z2+w3·Z3 → 1,305,792 total over 40 instances (absolute optimistic floor); (b) "relaxed tardiness LB" = merge all bays into one cumulative area resource, EDD list-schedule → ~98M total, per-instance LBs (prob_38 obj1 LB 2963, prob_27 LB 2031); (c) "mandatory peak/cap" oracle using [due-proc, release+proc] overlap windows — only 12-16 of 40 instances provably cannot reach Z1=0. Their identified next lever: "bay-assignment myopia" — greedy congests preferred bays; cross-bay rebalancing (paying w3 to save w1) is what they haven't built.
