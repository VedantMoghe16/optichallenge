## 2. Single most likely fatal flaw per design

**A — the master's optimism bias makes the Benders-flavored loop non-convergent, and its own escape hatch admits the machinery is dead weight.** Pseudo-costs ĉ_ij are learned only for *visited* (i,j) pairs; every unvisited bay keeps the "optimistic fluid marginal." The master therefore systematically ships blocks toward unexplored bays, the slaves discover the real tardiness, the pseudo-cost gets patched, and the master ships them somewhere else optimistic. No-good cuts on full assignment vectors exclude a measure-zero set; the trust region slows but does not fix the bias; κ-regression of tardiness on fluid backlog is fit on ~15 noisy points. The design's own mitigation — "if 3 consecutive master rounds fail, freeze a and go pure per-bay ALNS" — is what will actually ship, meaning ~2 of 6 weeks were spent building the component that gets frozen. Not a feasibility risk; a fatal opportunity-cost on a 4–6 week clock.

**B — schedule ossification under congestion: entries are frozen at insertion and repair is insertion-only, so coordinated re-timing does not exist as a move.** On peak-demand>1.0 instances (p25/p27/p33/p38/p40 — exactly where the objective mass is), the improving move is "push k blocks 2 days later and repack the hole," which under B is a chain of k independent uphill insertions that SA will essentially never sequence; the left-shift sweep is monotone one-directional and can't create the slack. Compounding: the reverse-crane EV scan is per-event-day inside the candidate interval — with P up to 31 and dense bays, dozens of event days × layers per candidate erode the 0.1–0.5 µs claim by 1–2 orders of magnitude precisely on the long-P instances, so the claimed 20–60 iter/s may be 2–6 iter/s where iterations matter most.

**C — the roofline calculus is sound only under the chronological-decode precondition, and the design violates it in two places.** Concrete counterexample: π places block X at [10,17); a later-π block M is then placed at [5,13) with M.layer1 overhanging X.layer0's position. I1 passes (same-layer only), I2 at day 5 passes (X absent from occ[5]), I3 passes (X absent from lowmass[5]) — yet X's Stage-2 entry at day 10 is broken (roof over it). The §4 `decode()` pseudocode is block-major with per-block `for t in R_i..` (out-of-order entries), and ALNS repair "re-derives ENTRY" mid-timeline — both break the theorem's precondition, not its conclusion. The correct fixes are (i) strictly day-major decoding plus (ii) suffix re-decode from the earliest touched day on every repair — which makes early-day moves cost a near-full bay re-decode and pushes real throughput well below the claimed 7–15 iter/s, concentrating search on late-day tinkering where the value isn't. (B's reverse-crane EV primitive is exactly the missing check; C lacks it.)

## 3. The winning hybrid — "KEEL": exact-raster unified LNS with decoder-repair and a bounded assignment seed

The synthesis judgment: **B's engine and search loop are the chassis; C's rasterizer and proofs are the geometry; A/C's assignment MIP survives only as a seed plus a narrow regime-gated re-solve; C's decoder survives as a repair operator inside B's ALNS.** The C-counterexample above is the load-bearing insight: B's reverse-crane primitive is what makes arbitrary-time insertion sound, so it subsumes C's chronology restriction — build everything on it.

**Geometry engine.** C's exact int64 scanline rasterizer (vertices ×10⁴, zero floating point anywhere; cell marked iff area(cell∩P)>0), S=4 default, congestion-triggered S=8 per hard block, S=2 memory degrade. B's storage: bit-packed uint64 rows with 64/S pre-shifted phase copies; per (block,orient,layer) masks + C's prefix-OR D_j + dilated contact masks; per (bay,day,layer) sparse day-grids stamped half-open; B's per (bay,day,layer) EV event masks. Four primitives, all packed AND: P1 same-layer interval-union (exact, B's proof); P2 entry crane in C's identity form occ[t][j] ∧ D_j (fewer word-ops than suffix unions); P3 exit crane vs day-grid[e−1]; **P4 reverse-crane EV scan over event days strictly inside (t,e)** — mandatory, per the counterexample. Containment via precomputed legal (x,y) ranges (reference vertex may be negative — fact 11). Escalation path for high-value raster rejections: shapely confirm with checker-identical arithmetic (translate-then-int, buffer(0), area>0), only when it kills a tardy day; fractional-fractional flush never emitted unverified (fact 14). Implementation ladder: **numba warm-cached first** (NUMBA_CACHE_DIR set, dummy-instance warmup inside `algorithm()`), prebuilt Ubuntu-24.04 .so as optional accelerator, fftconvolve/numpy as last resort. Build C++ only if Experiment E1 shows numba can't hit ≤5 µs/candidate.

**Representation.** B's flat arrays `bay[i], x[i], y[i], o[i], entry[i]`, `exit=entry+P` invariant; MLI op ordering (append ENTRY, prepend EXIT per day — validated in E2); incremental objective ledger with O(1) Z2/Z3 deltas and exact Z1.

**Construction.** Assignment seed = C's LOAD-relaxation Gurobi MIP (≤1500 binaries: w3 regret + linearized pairwise Z2 + bucket-overload with η=0.7, TimeLimit 1 s, warm fallback CP-SAT → greedy regret ranking). Then day-major ATC-space admission (priority exp(−slack⁺/(k·p̄))/(area^α P^β)), candidates from contact/skyline points on post-exit day states, scored by time-aware best-contact (exit-date similarity weighting) + diagonal fill + P4-priced overhang bonus (mushroom roofing over shorter-exit neighbors). SWO blame wrapper, 10–30 rounds, ~5% of budget.

**Search loop.** B's ALNS verbatim — destroy {blame/SWO, Shaw, time-window, spatial-cell, bay-flush, preference-repatriation, Z2-swap (regime-gated)}, repair {regret-2, most-constrained-first, randomized greedy}, adaptive weights, SA acceptance calibrated to 1-tardy-day-equivalent — **plus one new repair operator that is the whole point of the hybrid: DECODER-REPAIR.** Pick a bay and a day t0; unstamp every block in that bay with entry ≥ t0 (plus the destroyed set); re-run the day-major ATC-space decoder from t0 over the freed state (chronological ⇒ C's roofline theorems apply within the operator; the standing prefix is protected by P4). Cost ~50–300 ms; weight-adapted like any operator. This is the coordinated re-timing move that cures B's ossification, imports C's decoder soundness where it's valid, and delivers A's re-timing benefit with zero master machinery. Every K improvements: left-shift compaction sweep; every incumbent promotion: bundled `check_feasibility` gate (40–60 ms) + re-serialization.

**Where Gurobi enters — two bounded roles, nothing else.** (1) The construction assignment seed above. (2) Regime-gated re-solve: only when w3·gapMed/w1 > 3 (preference-locked: p32/p34/p37-like) or w2·(ΣL/m)/w1 > 5 (Z2-heavy: p25/p36/p40-like), the orchestrator re-solves the assignment every ~60 s with realized-tardiness pseudo-costs for *visited pairs only*, trust region Σ(1−x_{i,a*}) ≤ 0.05n, applied as a targeted bay-flush of the changed blocks; skipped entirely below 5-minute limits. A's fluid-backlog calibration loop (η/κ regression, no-goods) is cut. Fallback chain Gurobi → CP-SAT → exact-delta local search on a.

**Parallelization (4 cores).** Fork after construction, COW-shared masks, all thread env vars pinned to 1. Three ALNS replicas (2× SA with distinct seeds/cooling/operator mixes/κ; 1× record-to-record at 2% deviation) + one orchestrator doing checker gating, incumbent migration every ~15 s (migrant re-gated by checker before adoption), the regime-gated MIP re-solves, and serialization. Lagging replica (>10% behind for 60 s) reseeds from global best + 30% ruin. m=1: assignment ops disabled, replicas differ by seed/operators.

**Anytime/fallback safety.** t<1 s: fits-aware far-future serialized fallback (bay-search per block — fact 9's 7/300 lesson), held on disk. Deadline = start + timelimit − max(5 s, 3%); signal watchdog + explicit clock checks in every loop + global try/except, all returning the last checker-verified serialized incumbent. Canonical decimal-int time keys only; block-id/bay-id range asserts before emission (checker crashes on bad ids — fact 8). −1 is structurally impossible: nothing is ever emitted that hasn't passed the bundled checker.

**Budget:** 2% parse/triage/fallback, 5% construct+SWO, 88% ALNS, 5% final verify/emit; all schedules keyed to elapsed fraction.

## 4. Six-milestone roadmap (ordered by leaderboard value per effort)

**M1 — Safety floor (week 1, ~3 days).** Parser, fits-aware far-future fallback, canonical emitter, deadline watchdog, batch harness that runs all 40 training instances through `check_feasibility` and scores. *DoD:* every training instance returns a feasible dict at timelimits {60 s, 300 s, 1800 s} with zero crashes across 5 seeded runs; harness prints per-instance Z1/Z2/Z3 and objective. This alone guarantees never worse than rank-floor; everything after is upside.

**M2 — Geometry kernel (weeks 1–2).** Exact int rasterizer, packed masks/phases, day-grids, EV masks, primitives P1–P4, containment ranges, numba kernels warm-cached, shapely rescue path. *DoD:* differential test vs `utils.py` on ≥10⁵ random placements per training instance shows **zero** false-accepts; false-reject (incompleteness) rate measured and <5% of near-contact candidates at S=4; single candidate ≤5 µs, full-bay map ≤3 ms; memory <300 MB at n=300.

**M3 — Constructor + SWO (week 2–3).** MIP assignment seed (with CP-SAT/greedy fallbacks), day-major ATC-space decoder, contact/diagonal/overhang scoring, same-day reuse, SWO loop. *DoD:* beats baseline greedy's objective on ≥35/40 instances within 60 s; Z1=0 on ≥15/20 set1 instances; end-to-end checker pass rate 100%.

**M4 — Single-core unified ALNS (weeks 3–4).** MLI insertion move, destroy/repair portfolio including decoder-repair, SA acceptance, compaction sweeps, checker-gated promotions. *DoD:* ≥15 sustained iter/s at n=300 on p38 (worst case, long-P reverse-crane load); ≥15% mean objective improvement over M3 at 5 min on set2; 0 checker rejections of promoted incumbents over 40 instances × 3 seeds.

**M5 — Assignment layer + 4-core portfolio (weeks 4–5).** Cross-bay operators with exact exchange-rate tests, regime classifier, regime-gated MIP re-solve, 3-replica + orchestrator fork architecture with migration. *DoD:* set1: Z1=0 with Z3 within 5% of preference-argmax on ≥18/20; p32/p34/p37 objective improves ≥10% over frozen-assignment ablation; 4-core quality-at-5-min ≥ single-core quality-at-15-min.

**M6 — Hardening + report assets (weeks 5–6).** Adaptive S, sparse-grid memory governor, degraded modes (<90 s limits), full ladder exercised in an Ubuntu 24.04 container, fixed seeds per fingerprint; ablation runs (AABB→raster, EDD→ATC-space, no-overhang→P4-priced overhang, no-decoder-repair→full portfolio, frozen→searched assignment) and novelty writeup (reverse-crane calculus + MLI + decoder-repair-inside-LNS + exact-int rasterization safer than the evaluator's floats). *DoD:* container sweep of 40 instances × {180 s, 600 s, 1800 s} with zero crashes/timeouts; ablation table complete; report skeleton drafted.

## 5. Three de-risking experiments to run first

**E1 — Kernel soundness/incompleteness/throughput differential (gates M2 and the whole geometry bet).** Rasterize all 40 instances at S=4 and S=8; generate ≥10⁵ random (block, orient, x, y, bay, co-present set) configurations per instance biased toward near-contact; assert raster-accept ⇒ shapely-checker-accept (must be exactly 0 violations), measure false-reject rate vs S, and microbenchmark numba candidate tests and full-bay maps — including the reverse-crane EV scan cost on long-P dense states (p27/p38) to validate realized iter/s before committing to the numba-vs-C++ ladder and the S default.

**E2 — Stage-5 choreography / MLI / reverse-crane stress (gates M4's core invariant).** Programmatically generate thousands of randomized insertion sequences exercising same-day exit→entry reuse, nest construction/unwinding, boundary-equal nested intervals, and multi-block same-day cascades; emit under MLI and replay through the bundled `check_feasibility`. Include the C-counterexample class (out-of-order insertion with overhang over a later entrant) twice: without P4 (expect checker failure — proving the primitive is required) and with P4 (expect 100% pass). Any surviving failure is a semantics gap that must be fixed before the LNS exists.

**E3 — Value-of-information on the assignment dimension (gates the Gurobi scope).** On {p1, p8, p20, p27, p32, p40} (spanning loose/tight/preference-locked/Z2-heavy regimes), fix a 5-minute budget with the M3 constructor + a crude single-core LNS and compare: (i) preference-argmax assignment frozen, (ii) MIP seed frozen, (iii) MIP seed + cross-bay LNS operators, (iv) (iii) + one mid-run MIP re-solve. Measure final objective and Z1/Z2/Z3 split. This decides — with data, before week 3 — whether the regime-gated re-solve earns its complexity or whether exact-delta operators alone cover the assignment dimension (in which case Gurobi remains seed-only and the saved week goes to M4/M6).
