## 1. Continuous Berth Allocation Problem (BAP) — closest studied relative

Isomorphism: vessel = rectangle in (quay-position × time), arrival = release, handling = processing, objective = weighted waiting/tardiness. Our per-bay subproblem is continuous BAP + a second spatial dimension + polygons + crane/LIFO coupling.

**Dominant pattern A — ALNS in the space-time diagram** (Ropke–Pisinger style; best current results on continuous/multi-port BAP). Concrete design from the multi-port continuous BAP ALNS ([arXiv 2302.02356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02356), EJOR 2024; builds on Iris et al. 2017 BACAP ALNS):
- *Removal ops (remove K = ρ·n related items — unrelated removals are wasted)*: (i) **Shaw relatedness** M_ij = A|x_i−x_j| + B|start_i−start_j| + C|end_i−end_j|, greedy-randomized selection via index ⌈|Ω|·p^α⌉; (ii) **cost-time / cost-space removal**: pick the most expensive assignment (its tardiness/waiting cost), remove it plus all neighbors overlapping it in time (resp. space); (iii) pure random removal for diversification.
- *Repair ops*: (i) **randomized greedy insertion ordered by fewest feasible insertion positions** (most-constrained-first, not EDD!); (ii) **regret-κ insertion** (Potvin–Rousseau): plan the item with largest cost gap between best and κ-th-best position; (iii) **packing greedy insertion**: restrict candidate positions to those *strictly adjacent* to an already-placed rectangle or to the boundary of the quay/horizon (corner/contact points) — explicitly to avoid unusable slivers; (iv) **arrival greedy insertion** (earliest-arrival-first, deliberately suboptimal cost to unlock positions for others).
- *Acceptance*: simulated annealing with T_start→T_end and geometric cooling; adaptive operator weights with 4 reward tiers (new best / better / accepted-worse / rejected).
- Also: an [ALNS for discrete+continuous BAP](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0305054816000046) beat prior best-known bounds; [GRASP for continuous BAP](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1366554510000207) = RCL-randomized construction + local search, weaker than ALNS on recent benchmarks.

**Dominant pattern B — search over priority lists, not geometry** ([Meisel & Bierwirth 2009](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1366554508000768)): a greedy time-space construction decodes a vessel priority list; SWO (adjacent swaps driven by blame) and tabu search (pairwise swaps) operate purely on the list. This "permutation + decoder" architecture is the standard way to keep geometry out of the neighborhood.

**Mapping**: our decoder = per-bay (place earliest feasible day, adjacency-candidate (x,y)); metaheuristic state = (assignment vector, per-bay priority order). Removal ops translate directly: time-window removal (all blocks with ENTRY in [t1,t2] in one bay), spatial-cluster removal, worst-tardiness removal, related removal by (due, area, bay-pref) similarity.

## 2. Strip packing / Dynamic Storage Allocation (time axis)

- **DSA** = pack rectangles where the time interval is *fixed* and only the address (spatial coordinate) is chosen: [approximation algorithms for DSA](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/3-540-61680-2_46) (Gergov 3-approx; Buchsbaum et al. 2+ε) all use **boxing**: round sizes to size classes, group small items into boxes, allocate boxes hierarchically; **LOAD lower bound** — max over t of total size alive at t — is within a constant of OPT, and [First-Fit is within ~7% of optimal on average](https://www.academia.edu/65894838/Efficient_implementation_of_the_first_fit_strategy_for_dynamic_storage_allocation) despite poor worst case. Dual problem: [Demand Strip Packing](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol207-approx-random2021/LIPIcs.APPROX-RANDOM.2021.20/LIPIcs.APPROX-RANDOM.2021.20.pdf) — durations fixed, choose start times, minimize peak resource.
- **Mapping (two exploits)**: (a) the LOAD bound gives a *cheap capacity relaxation*: per bay, per time bucket, per layer, sum of alive polygon areas ≤ η·bay_area (η≈0.6–0.8 packing efficiency) — use it in the assignment master and as a congestion signal without any geometry; (b) once ENTRY/EXIT are tentatively fixed, placement is 2D-DSA → first-fit/bottom-left over **corner/adjacency candidates** is the right primitive; and because the checker rounds to integer coordinates and bays are ≤ ~180×29, per-(bay, layer, day) **occupancy bitmaps with bitwise-AND collision tests (numpy/numba)** replace shapely in the inner loop entirely (shapely only for final verification). Best-contact / touching-perimeter scoring from the nesting literature ([shipbuilding BCA + GA over sequence](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0360835220306550)) breaks ties toward dense packing.

## 3. Parallel-machine total (weighted) tardiness — priority rules

- **ATC** (apparent tardiness cost): I_j(t) = (w_j/p_j)·exp(−(d_j − p_j − t)^+/(k·p̄)); interpolates WSPT↔EDD; k tuned to due-date tightness/range. With release dates, **ATCR/ATCSR** add an exp(−(r_j−t)^+/k₂p̄) factor and [dominate plain ATC/EDD with unequal ready times](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S092552731200028X); [workload-adaptive lookahead k improves ATC further](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0925527306002118). COVERT is the older alternative; ATC-family wins in comparisons.
- **Mapping**: the scarce resource is bay *space-time*, so the correct denominator is not p_i but the block's space-time volume: priority_i(t) ∝ exp(−slack_i(t)/k·p̄) / (area_i · P_i). Decode day-by-day: at each day, admit released blocks in ATC order while the bitmap accepts them. EDD (the baseline) is the k→0 limit; ATC's exponential slack discount is precisely what stops one urgent-but-huge block from starving several small urgent ones. Since w1 is a single global weight, this is total tardiness — weights drop out but the space-normalization stays the differentiator.

## 4. Squeaky Wheel Optimization & Iterated Greedy — the backbone

- **SWO** ([Joslin & Clements, JAIR 1999](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/1998/AAAI98-048.pdf)): Construct (greedy over priority list) → Analyze (blame = each element's measured contribution to cost, e.g., its tardiness) → Prioritize (promote blamed elements). Searches priority-space and solution-space simultaneously; makes large coherent moves; proven on production scheduling and on BAP itself (Meisel–Bierwirth). [Evolutionary SWO](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/224342954_An_Evolutionary_Squeaky_Wheel_Optimization_Approach_to_Personnel_Scheduling) adds a population and selection over priority vectors.
- **Iterated Greedy** ([Ruiz & Stützle 2007](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0377221705008507), also the [setup+weighted-tardiness variant](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0377221706008277)): remove d elements (random or biased), greedily re-insert each at its best position, accept with constant-temperature SA. State of the art in flowshop for a decade; ~2 parameters. IG and ALNS are formally the same loop; SWO supplies the *smart* destroy: blame_i = w1·tardiness_i + w3·pref_regret_i (+ imbalance marginal), remove/promote the top-blamed.
- **Mapping**: this is the cheapest high-yield upgrade over the given baseline: keep the greedy decoder, wrap it in Construct/Analyze/Prioritize with ATC-initialized priorities, add IG-style biased destruction + SA acceptance. Robust, anytime, trivially parallel (4 independent chains or 1 chain per bay after assignment freeze).

## 5. LIFO / stacking-precedence constraints (crane rule)

- **PDP with LIFO loading** ([branch-and-cut, Cordeau et al.](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220209200_A_branch-and-cut_algorithm_for_the_Pickup_and_Delivery_Traveling_Salesman_Problem_with_LIFO_loading); [additive B&B](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/274608184_An_additive_branch-and-bound_algorithm_for_the_pickup_and_delivery_traveling_salesman_problem_with_LIFO_or_FIFO_loading)): feasible LIFO solutions = properly *nested* intervals (a forest); specialized neighborhoods operate on the nesting tree, never breaking nestedness.
- **Container Relocation Problem**: count of "badly placed" (blocking) items = lower bound and blame signal; the golden stacking rule is **place X on top of Y only if X leaves no later than Y** — then zero reshuffles.
- **Mapping**: our crane rule (at A's EXIT, A's layer k must be clear of all co-present layers j ≥ k) means: if B's upper layer overhangs A, then A cannot exit while B is present ⇒ induced constraint EXIT_A ≥ EXIT_B (or forego). Adopt the CRP rule as a *construction invariant*: **permit an overhang of B over A only if planned EXIT_B ≤ planned EXIT_A** (due-date/exit-monotone stacking). This keeps the induced precedence digraph trivially satisfied, costs nothing to check on bitmaps (compare planned exits), and converts the hardest constraint into a placement filter. In improvement phases, count violated/tight overhang pairs as CRP-style blame; only relax the invariant deliberately for dense end-game packing.

## 6. Direct domain literature (shipbuilding spatial scheduling)

[Journal of Scheduling 2024 spatiotemporal block assembly](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10951-024-00804-1): two-phase matheuristic on 637–810-block industrial instances — **Phase I: MIP for timing + bay assignment using areas only (capacity relaxation, no geometry); Phase II: per-bay placement IPs solved independently in parallel**, with outsourcing (≈ our far-future fallback) as recourse. Validates exactly the decomposition your structural fact (3) suggests. Earlier Korean work ([DAS-CURVE](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0957417496000103), [BCA + GA](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0360835220306550), [SRCPSP B&B](https://www.hindawi.com/journals/mpe/2015/628259/)) confirms: sequence-driven placement + best-contact scoring is the domain standard; nobody exploits the crane/LIFO structure explicitly — that is an open novelty slot.

## Proposed hybrid template (grounded in the above)

**State** = (bay assignment vector a, per-bay priority list π_j). **Decoder** (per bay, parallel over 4 cores): day-major ATC-ordered admission; candidate (x,y) = corner/adjacency points on integer occupancy bitmaps per (layer, day); score = lexicographic (entry day, LIFO-safe overhang bonus if EXIT-monotone, best-contact perimeter, low y); EXIT = ENTRY + P (dwell only if crane-blocked); reuse same-day EXIT space.

1. **Master (assignment)**: Z2+Z3 are exact functions of a → solve a small MILP/min-cost assignment (Gurobi, seconds) minimizing w2·imbalance + w3·regret + λ·(estimated tardiness from capacity relaxation: per-bay per-bucket LOAD vs η·area), warm-started each round with congestion duals/blame from slaves — Benders-flavored, novel for this problem class.
2. **Slaves**: decode each bay; report per-block tardiness + congestion buckets.
3. **Improvement loop (ALNS/IG/SWO fusion)**: destroy = {worst-blame (SWO), Shaw-related (|Δdue|,|Δarea|,same bay), time-window, spatial-cluster, bay-reassign of blamed cluster}; repair = {greedy most-constrained-first, regret-2}; acceptance = SA with adaptive operator weights (4-tier rewards); occasional master re-solve with updated λ/capacities.
4. **Safety**: always retain best *checker-verified* incumbent; guaranteed-feasible serialization fallback; hard time budget with anytime cutoff.

Expected edge over field: bitmap collision engine (speed → more iterations), EXIT-monotone stacking invariant (CRP import, handles the constraint everyone else will fight), exact Z2/Z3 master (decomposition exploit), ATC-per-space-time priority (beats EDD baselines).
